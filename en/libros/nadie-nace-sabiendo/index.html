Unpublished novel · comic fantasy · grief

Nadie nace sabiendo

An as-yet unpublished novel: a fantastic comedy about the dead returning, monsters carrying on, and characters discovering that not even death resolves attachment.

The metaphysical becomes domestic: zombies with routines, vampires with opinions and paperwork for managing the impossible. Beneath the comedy, the novel deals with loss, the difficulty of letting go and the persistence of bonds when they should no longer be able to endure.
